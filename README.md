### Featured projects
* [**Global Sports League Scheduling**](https://github.com/TY-PEric/IMMC-Global-Sports-Scheduling-Optimization) - **IMMC 2025 Global Finalist**, algorithm lead. AHP team selection, a 10,000-season Monte Carlo comparison of five league formats (Bradley-Terry match model, Jensen-Shannon divergence for fairness), and an NSGA-II genetic algorithm that schedules the group stage to keep travel short and even across teams. Defended in Hong Kong.
* [**HPC Environmental Impact Model**](https://github.com/TY-PEric/HiMCM-Data-Center-Carbon-Emissions) - **HiMCM 2024 Honorable Mention**, lead modeler. Component-level energy model and life-cycle carbon assessment of HPC systems, GM(1,1) grey forecasting of PUE, and a GPU upgrade simulation projecting emissions to 2030.
* [**Emerging Careers Model**](https://github.com/TY-PEric/IMMC-Career-Prospect-Predictive-Model) - **IMMC 2025 qualifying round**. TOPSIS rankings of China's 19 new professions with coefficient-of-variation and three-layer AHP weights, plus a survey that recommends careers to graduates.
* [**Sign Language Recognition Reproduction**](https://github.com/TY-PEric/YuanPei_Young_Scholar_Program) - Yuanpei Young Scholars computer vision program. Reproduced the SL-GCN and SSTCN skeleton-based models from SAM-SLR with the authors' code and checkpoints on a Tencent Cloud server.
